# RunTeams.ai logo design contract

## Experience principle

The logo should feel like a quiet piece of product infrastructure: clear at first glance, distinctive on the second, and never louder than the work shown beside it.

## Selected visual direction

**Team Rail** is a compact symbol-plus-wordmark identity. Three independent input rails converge into one controlled terminal, representing multiple complete Agent Runtimes coordinated as a single team. It remains unboxed in the website header, so it reads as a brand lockup rather than an app icon.

Owner approval recorded on 2026-08-21. Team Rail is now the production direction for the website and product brand mark.

## Alternatives retained for review

- **Relay Cut:** a wordmark with a small handoff marker between `Run` and `Teams`.
- **RT Frame:** a compact `RT` monogram built from the same stroke weight as the wordmark.

## Typography

- Register: restrained neo-grotesque/humanist sans.
- Review font: Arial/Helvetica system stack for license-safe local rendering.
- Weight: 700 for `RunTeams`, 500 for `.ai`.
- Tracking: slightly negative at display size, neutral at navigation size.
- Capitalization: `RunTeams.ai`; do not use all caps or all lowercase in the primary mark.
- Production refinement: after direction approval, outline the wordmark and optically adjust `unT`, `Te`, and `ms.` pairs.

## Color

- Ink: `#171716`
- Warm canvas: `#F7F6F3`
- Secondary suffix: `#77736C`
- Reverse: `#FFFFFF` on `#171716`
- The mark must remain valid in a single color; muted `.ai` is optional, not structural.

## Geometry

- Primary lockup height: 28 px.
- Clear space: at least the cap-height of the lowercase `i` around all sides.
- Minimum wordmark width: 112 px without `.ai`, 132 px with `.ai`.
- Minimum standalone symbol: 16 px.
- Symbol and wordmark optical gap: 9–11 px at a 28 px mark height.

## Do / don't

- Do keep the mark flat, one-color capable, and horizontally efficient.
- Do test against actual navigation labels and CTA buttons.
- Do let the name carry recognition.
- Don't place it in a rounded-square container in the website header.
- Don't add gradients, glow, dots, robot/person pictograms, or extra arrows.
- Don't use the symbol-only fallback as the public website logo before recognition exists.
