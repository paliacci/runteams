# Render critique

## Pass 1 — observed failures

The first 1600×1120 render was inspected at both hero and 28 px navigation sizes.

1. **Relay Cut read as damage, not handoff.** Its connector crossed the `n/T` join and looked like a deletion line at navigation size.
2. **The initially selected direction ignored accepted feedback.** Relay Cut remained too close to the previous wordmark-only round, which the owner had already rejected.
3. **The strongest website hierarchy was not the selected one.** Team Rail kept the product name immediately readable while adding a compact, unboxed symbol that survived the 28 px and reverse tests.
4. **A symbol-led risk remains.** Team Rail could become a generic routing arrow if a pointed terminal or extra motion detail is added.

## Revisions made

- Moved the Relay Cut marker into a dedicated gap and gave it a small chevron, eliminating the strike-through collision.
- Promoted Team Rail to the selected direction and updated the scorecard, design contract, provenance manifest, and real-header tests.
- Kept the Team Rail terminal neutral and rounded; no arrowhead, enclosing square, gradient, or color dependency was introduced.
- Retained RT Frame only as an alternative compact architecture rather than merging it into the primary mark.

## Pass 2 — result

The second render preserves immediate word recognition in light and dark headers, maintains a compact 28 px silhouette, and represents the product through structure rather than illustration. The remaining work after owner selection is optical path refinement and converting live type to final outlined wordforms.
