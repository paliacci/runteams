# RunTeams.ai website logo brief

## Product truth

- Product: RunTeams.ai
- Category: local-first AI team orchestration built on complete Agent Runtimes.
- Core promise: bring existing agent subscriptions and organize them into a working team.
- Primary context: website header at 24–32 px high; secondary contexts are product sidebar, favicon, reverse-on-dark, and one-color print.

## What the logo must signal

- One operator can coordinate multiple capable agents.
- Work moves through a controlled team rather than an uncontrolled AI swarm.
- The product is calm, precise, durable, and credible enough for daily work.

## What it must reject

- App-icon-first presentation, mascots, robots, literal people, sparkles, chat bubbles, neural-network dots, gradients, or generic startup loops.
- A symbol that needs an explanation at navigation size.
- Decorative details that disappear at 16–24 px.

## Frozen application tests

1. Primary website lockup: 176–206 px wide, 28 px high.
2. Compact product lockup: 132–160 px wide, 22 px high.
3. Symbol fallback: 16 px and 32 px.
4. Pure black on warm white and pure white on near-black.
5. Legible when the `.ai` suffix is omitted in tight product UI.
