# Direction decision

## Selected direction: 02 — Team Rail

Team Rail is selected for refinement because it provides a recognizable 24–28 px website mark without putting the identity inside an app-icon container. Three independent rails converge into one controlled output, which maps directly to RunTeams.ai's product truth: multiple complete Agent Runtimes coordinated as one working team. The symbol remains secondary to the readable name in the public lockup.

**Decision status:** approved by the owner on 2026-08-21 and promoted to the production website and product brand mark.

## Rejected as primary

- **01 Relay Cut:** the wordmark reads immediately, but the first render showed that its handoff stroke becomes too close to a deletion line at navigation size. More importantly, it remains close to the wordmark-only territory already rejected in the previous round.
- **03 RT Frame:** the monogram is compact and useful in product chrome, but `RT` has many existing associations and needs the wordmark to become recognizable. It is better as a product badge than the first website impression.

## Risk and next decision

The symbol can resemble a routing or merge glyph if its proportions become too mechanical. Production refinement must preserve three clearly independent inputs and a neutral terminal rather than exaggerating an arrowhead. Do not average all three directions into a hybrid.
