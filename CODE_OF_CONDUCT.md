# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

RunTeams is built by people with different backgrounds and experience levels. We expect participants to be respectful, direct, patient, and open to correction.

Constructive disagreement is welcome. Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, and publishing someone else's private information are not.

## Applying the standard

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or limit participation when behavior violates this standard. They will aim to explain decisions clearly and consistently.

Report serious or private concerns to `conduct@runteams.ai`. For security vulnerabilities, use the process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
